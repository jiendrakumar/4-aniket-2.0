The KENT Advantage

Patented technologies delivering purity, health, and zero water wastage

Mineral RO™ Technology

KENT's patented Mineral RO™ Technology uses a TDS Controller that retains essential natural minerals in purified water, making it 100% pure and healthy.

Copper-infused filtration media

Recirculates rejected water back to the overhead tank using its own internal pump, ensuring not a single drop of water is wasted during purification.

In-Tank UV Disinfection

Keeps purified water in the storage tank free from microbial contamination for long durations with automatic LED UV light protection.

Digital Display & IoT

Real-time status display showing water purity, filter life indicator, RO flow rate, and auto-fault alerts on an interactive screen.

Stainless Steel Storage

Selected models feature high-grade food stainless steel storage tanks preventing bacterial growth and maintaining purity.

Multiple Purification

Combines RO + UV + UF + TDS Control + In-Tank UV to eliminate dissolved impurities like heavy metals, arsenic, pesticides, and germs.
